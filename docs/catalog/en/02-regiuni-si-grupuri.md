#### 10. Girandola (`girandola`)

**One more group of 9 cells in a pinwheel shape.**

On top of the classic rules, the nine marked cells (the four corners, the centre and the four cells one step inside the midpoint of each side: r2c5, r5c2, r5c8, r8c5) must hold 1–9 once each. This is how Girandola is described in Regexp::Sudoku (the pinwheel shape); other sources do not give exact coordinates. 9×9 only.

#### 11. Scattered nine (`sparse9`)

**One more group of 9 cells: corners, side midpoints, centre.**
