<a id="antiknight"></a>
#### 16. Anti-knight (`antiknight`)

**No equal digits a knight's move apart.**

In addition to the classic rules, two cells a chess knight's move apart cannot contain the same digit. 9×9 only.

<a id="antiking"></a>
#### 17. Anti-king (`antiking`)

**Diagonal neighbours differ.**

In addition to the classic rules, two diagonally adjacent cells (a king's move) cannot contain the same digit. 9×9 only.

<a id="nonconsec"></a>
#### 18. Non-consecutive (`nonconsec`)

**Neighbours are never consecutive.**

In addition to the classic rules, orthogonally adjacent cells cannot contain consecutive digits (for example 4 next to 5). 9×9 and 6×6.

<table><tr><td align="center"><img src="../../screens/nonconsec_a.webp" width="300" alt="Non-consecutive: starting puzzle"><br><sub>Starting puzzle (9×9 · medium · 24 givens)</sub></td><td align="center"><img src="../../screens/nonconsec_b.webp" width="300" alt="Non-consecutive: after 4 steps"><br><sub>After 4 “Step by step” steps</sub></td></tr></table>

<a id="miracle"></a>
#### 19. Miracle (`miracle`)

**Anti-knight + anti-king + non-consecutive.**

All three restrictions above at once. The variant is extremely restrictive: puzzles have very few givens.
