_The small numbers in the corner of a cage or on the edge are sums; digits in a cage do not repeat unless the rules sheet says otherwise._

<a id="killer"></a>
#### 38. Killer (`killer`)

**Cages with sums, no repeats.**

The grid is divided into dashed cages; the number in the corner is the sum of the digits in the cage. A digit cannot repeat inside a cage.

#### 39. Little Killer (`littlekiller`)

**Diagonal sum from the edge.**

A number with an arrow outside the grid is the sum of the digits on the diagonal it points along; digits may repeat on the diagonal.

<a id="luk"></a>
#### 40. Little Unique Killer (`luk`)

**Little Killer with distinct digits on the diagonal.**

The number next to a slanted arrow is the sum of the digits on the diagonal it points along, and on each such diagonal all digits are different (the difference from classic Little Killer). The name “Little Unique Killer” is this app’s own; I found no published variant under that name.
