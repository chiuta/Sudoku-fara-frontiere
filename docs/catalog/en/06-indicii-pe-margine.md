#### 41. Sandwich (`sandwich`)

**Sum between 1 and the largest digit.**

The number outside is the sum of the digits between the 1 and the digit N (9 in a 9×9 grid) in that row or column.

<a id="rossini"></a>
#### 45. Rossini (`rossini`)

**Edge arrow: the first three digits rise one way.**

An arrow outside the grid says the first three digits from that end are strictly monotone: they increase in the direction of the arrow (into the grid) or, if the arrow points towards the edge, they increase towards the edge, i.e. decrease going inwards. Convention: the arrowhead points to the largest digit. A missing arrow means nothing (this is not the classic Rossini form, where ends without an arrow rule out monotone runs).

#### 47. Radar (`radar`)

**The clue tells where the biggest digit is.**

In addition to the classic rules, a number outside the grid gives the position (counted from that end) of the largest digit (9 at 9×9) in that row or column.
