**Clues appear as you solve.**

#### 87. Steps · Steps (`steps`)

**Signed differences keep growing.**

In addition to the classic rules, on a steps line (the arrow shows the direction) the difference between neighbouring digits grows from step to step, counting signed differences (a₃−a₂ > a₂−a₁): 1-2-4 and 9-5-3 are allowed, 4-3-1 is not. The line may also run diagonally; two consecutive cells can hold the same digit only if they share no row, column or box.
